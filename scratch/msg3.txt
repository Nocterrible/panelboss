window: the edge descriptor rides every monitor identity - one seam, suppressed when it carries no news

desk_position's owner law (Oct 22): each descriptor COMPONENT shows
iff at least one row in the LISTED SET differs on it - an all-'top
left' desk says nothing, a side-by-side desk drops the vacuous 'top'
and says ' [left]' / ' [right]', a single-row card says nothing, and
before any desktop capture has answered (empty row set) the slot is
ABSENT, not a dash. It is a pure function over the rows the card
lists, so suppression is per-context by construction.

The seam (monitor_row_label, _widgets) is the ONE place a monitor
identity becomes UI text: short name, duplicate-model gdi tail, and
the descriptor. Routed through it: the away card's per-monitor rows
(already on the geometry word), the hider strip rows, the jog rows,
the dimmer rows, the theater's per-monitor rows, the gaming rate rows
(both menus), and the profiles tab's add-monitor menu (the captured
panel rows get pos=desk_position over their OWN profile's displays -
the profiles tab refreshes the pane on every capture, and the mode
rows rebuild there, so the descriptor populates with the capture and
is simply absent before it). The tray submenu builds its monitor
lines by hand off the pump thread - named follow-up, not today's
batch.
